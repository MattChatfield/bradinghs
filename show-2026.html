Overview

Show: Brading Horticultural Society Show 2026

Admission fee: Adults 20p

Venue: The Town Hall, Brading

Key times: Exhibits may be staged from 8pm to 9pm on Friday evening and from 7:30am to 10:30am on the morning of the show. Prizes are presented at 3:30pm.

Allergen warning: There will be produce in the show that contains nuts.

Exhibitor Information

Entry deadline: Entries by Friday 4th September. Entry fee per class is 30p and must accompany the entry form.

Entry addresses: 15a Station Gardens or The Town Hall, Brading.

Contact: Jo Horn on 07895 540494.

Main exhibitor rules

  • Exhibitors must reside in the parish of Brading, Alverstone or Yaverland.
  • All exhibits must be home grown produce with the exception of flowers for the Floral and Art sections, Classes 69–73.
  • All Section 4 exhibits must display a list of ingredients.
  • No exhibitor may stage more than one exhibit in each class.
  • The Committee reserves the right to make more or fewer awards as the judge may think fit.
  • Wine must be openable.
  • Stewards and committee members only are allowed in the hall while judging is taking place.
  • Exhibits not according to the schedule will be disqualified.
  • Class entrance fee is 30p per entry and must accompany the entry form.
  • No exhibit may be removed before 4:30pm without permission of the Secretary.
  • All exhibits must be cleared by 5pm on the evening of the show.
  • Vegetables must be presented as 5 of each variety unless otherwise stated.
  • No appeal against the judge’s awards will be entertained unless a deposit of £1 is accompanied by a written protest before 3:30pm on the day of the show.
  • The Committee reserves the right to inspect gardens after receipt of entries.
  • Points awarded in classes are: 1st = 3, 2nd = 2, 3rd = 1.

Guidelines

General staging advice

  • Study the schedule carefully; it gives details of the classes, rules and conditions.
  • Fill in your entry form accurately. The class number must be correct.
  • Allow yourself enough time on the morning of the show.
  • When delivering exhibits, collect your exhibitor number from the show secretary.
  • Cards bearing your number will be placed face-down on the table under your entry.
  • Class numbers will be at the front of the bench where you should place your entry in accordance with the schedule.

Flower advice

  • Vases are supplied for Section 1 classes.
  • Ensure vases of flowers have sufficient water.
  • Cut flowers and foliage early morning or late evening when it is cool.
  • Cut stems as long as possible with a slanting cut to assist uptake of water.
  • Put flowers into water as soon as possible.
  • Remove any damaged flowers, broken stems and discoloured leaves.
  • Do not exhibit damaged or diseased fruit and vegetables.

Vegetable advice

  • Wash and clean root vegetables with a soft sponge; never use a brush.
  • Do not use oil or grease on vegetables.
  • Beetroots, carrots and turnips should have leaves shortened to not less than 7.5cms (3”).
  • Onions should be shown without tops and must not be skinned.
  • Runner beans should be displayed with flower stalks attached.
  • Tomatoes should be uniform in size, firm, with a green stalk intact on each.

Floral art advice

  • Flowers may be purchased for Section 8 classes.
  • Accessories are anything other than plant material used as part of the exhibit, except bases, containers and drapes.
  • Drapes are fabric or material placed behind or in association with an exhibit and are not considered accessories.
  • Plant materials should predominate in arrangements.
  • Keep accessories in scale and keep the exhibit simple.
  • Make sure arrangements have sufficient water.
  • Use the space allowed well and ensure the exhibit interprets the class title.

Class 50: Men Only Bread Pudding

Ingredients:

  • 500g white bread
  • 500g mixed fruit
  • 1 tbsp mixed spice
  • 600ml milk
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 140g brown sugar
  • 100g butter
  • 2 tbsp demerara sugar

Method: Tear the bread into a large mixing bowl, add the fruit and spice. Pour in the milk then scrunch through your fingers to mix everything together and break up the bread. Add eggs and demerara sugar, stir well and leave to soak for 15 minutes. Heat oven to 180°C/160°C fan. Butter and line a 20cm square cake tin. Stir the butter into the mixture, tip into tin, scatter demerara sugar on top. Bake in oven for 1½ hours until firm and golden.
